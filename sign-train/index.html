Sign Train

The engine rolls along the x axis and stops at every root. Each car carries one factor and shows that factor’s sign at the engine’s position. Multiply the cars together and you get the sign in the smoke.

Change the function

Watch a car flash when the engine crosses its root: that factor just changed sign. A squared factor never shows a minus, so the smoke keeps its sign there. The train comes in from the left and leaves on the right, because the domain is every real number. Drag the train or tap the track to jump to any spot. With the track selected, the arrow keys nudge the train and the space bar starts or pauses it.
